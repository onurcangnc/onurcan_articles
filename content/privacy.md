## 2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. This Site uses cookies for:

| Cookie Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Analytics cookies** | Google Analytics uses cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) to distinguish unique users and track sessions. |
| **Advertising cookies** | Third-party ad services may set cookies to deliver relevant advertisements. |

### Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect your browsing experience. Most browsers allow you to:

- View and delete individual cookies
- Block third-party cookies
- Block all cookies from specific sites
- Clear all cookies when you close the browser

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right of access** - request a copy of data we hold about you
- **Right to rectification** - request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** - request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restrict processing** - request that we limit how we use your data
- **Right to data portability** - receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object** - object to data processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.
